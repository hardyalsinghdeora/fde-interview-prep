# My Prep — Personal Profile, Stories and Answers

This folder turns the generic question bank into **my** answers, using my real career record.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [PROFILE.md](PROFILE.md) | My background, strengths, gaps, and which roles fit me best |
| [STORY-BANK.md](STORY-BANK.md) | My 12 core interview stories in STAR form, ready to adapt to any behavioral question |
| [MY-ANSWERS.md](MY-ANSWERS.md) | My personalised answers to the most-asked questions, plus a map from every behavioral question to the story to use |
| [TARGET-HIGHLEVEL.md](TARGET-HIGHLEVEL.md) | HighLevel Staff Engineer (CRM backend) openings ranked by fit, my gaps with learning materials, and HighLevel-style questions with model answers |
| [INPUTS-NEEDED.md](INPUTS-NEEDED.md) | What only I can fill in: missing details, numbers and stories. **Fill this first** |

## Privacy rules for this folder (the repo is public)

These files follow a simple rule: **the shape of the work is safe to publish, the names and numbers are not.**

- No employer, product, customer, school or colleague names.
- No internal table, schema, service or repo names, and no ticket or page IDs.
- No internal prices, targets, volumes or pilot counts.
- In interviews you can say more, as long as it's not confidential. Keep a private copy with the real details offline, not in this repo.

## Claim grades used in these files

| Grade | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **[confirmed]** | Backed by documents I wrote | Say it confidently |
| **[partial]** | True, but shared work or narrower than it sounds | Use the narrower wording given |
| **[needs input]** | I believe it's true but there's no evidence or number yet | Fill in [INPUTS-NEEDED.md](INPUTS-NEEDED.md) before using it |

A claim that falls apart under a follow-up question costs more than a modest claim that holds. Use the grades.
